Subsurface profile bands: layout prototype

Which extra graphs can be shown below the dive profile at the same time, in what order, and how much of the height they may take. Built for subsurface#5136 (a SAC trace) and the change that lets heart rate and the tissue heat map be shown together. The data are real Subsurface profile exports; the drawing is an approximation of Subsurface's, written for this page. Every view has its own URL, so a link shows exactly what you are looking at.

The page opens on the proposed layout. Decisions, below, lists what was considered for each choice, with the reasons for the options not chosen.

Decisions

Each question lists the options considered. Show sets the prototype to that option. Verdicts and reasons are filled in as we decide, so that the options we did not choose stay visible with the reason.

Source: github.com/notionparallax/subsurface-profile-bands. Not part of Subsurface. Colours follow core/color.cpp; band fractions follow ProfileScene::updateAxes().
