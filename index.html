Subsurface profile bands: layout prototype

Which extra graphs can be shown below the dive profile at the same time, in what order, and how much of the height they may take. Built for subsurface#5136 (a SAC trace) and the change that lets heart rate and the tissue heat map be shown together. The data are real Subsurface profile exports; the drawing is an approximation of Subsurface's, written for this page. It opens on the proposed layout, and every view has its own URL, so a link shows exactly what you are looking at.

Source: github.com/notionparallax/subsurface-profile-bands. Not part of Subsurface. Colours follow core/color.cpp; band fractions follow ProfileScene::updateAxes().
